# Acquisition Mandate v0.1

## 1. Long-term vision
Describe the type of technology-enabled industrial group we intend to build.

## 2. Initial industry focus
List the first two or three adjacent sectors where we possess genuine credibility.

## 3. Target business characteristics
Define the commercial, operational and management characteristics we seek.

## 4. Exclusion criteria
Define the conditions that would cause us to reject a business.

## 5. Fundamental assessment
Assess revenue, customers, margins, cash flow, management, competitive advantage and downside.

## 6. Technology and AI value-creation levers
Identify the repeatable technologies and operational interventions we can deploy.

## 7. People and deployment model
Explain how domain specialists, deployment engineers and AI systems work together.

## 8. Capital strategy
Describe how the first acquisition could be financed without relying entirely on venture capital.

## 9. First proof point
Identify the project, customer or operating business that could demonstrate the model.

## 10. Five-year outcome
Describe what must be true for the group to own and improve multiple businesses.
